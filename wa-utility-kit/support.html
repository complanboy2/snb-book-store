Get help with WA Utility Kit

Use this page for billing, login, manual activation, deleted-message history, new-number chat, and broadcast readiness issues for WA Utility Kit.

Email support · Install or open the Chrome Web Store listing

Before you write

  • Use the same email. Paid access is tied to the email used in the extension billing flow.
  • Include the version. Send your extension version and the exact screen or action that failed.
  • Add context. For UI issues, include a screenshot. For billing issues, include your payment email.

Common support paths

  • Billing and login. Use your original payment email through the in-extension login flow to restore access.
  • Manual activation. Use support activation only when WA Utility Kit support explicitly gives you a manual key.
  • Contact support. Email support@wawebplus.com with your payment email and issue summary.
  • Message any number. If you enter a local number without a country code, the extension uses your logged-in linked chat number for context.
  • Deleted history. Deleted-message records appear in Settings when the feature is enabled and can be cleaned there.
  • Bulk sending. Keep the chat workspace open and connected while sending. WA Utility Kit reports failures instead of pretending a send worked.

Support checklist

Include the email you used for payment or login, your extension version, and the exact screen or action that failed.

Attach screenshots for UI problems, deleted-message history problems, and any case where the screen state matters.

For bulk or broadcast issues, mention whether the chat workspace was open, connected, and ready at send time.

The most useful support request describes what you expected, what happened instead, where it happened, and which account or linked chat number was involved.
