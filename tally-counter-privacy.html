Tally Counter: Privacy Policy

Effective date: 24 November 2026

App: Tally Counter (package com.dwarkalabs.tally), published on Google Play by Dwarka Labs.

Tally Counter does not collect, store on any server, share or sell any personal or device data.

  • The app has no internet access. It does not request the INTERNET permission or any other Android permission.
  • The only information the app keeps is the number you are counting and the optional label you type. Both are saved privately on your own device using Android's standard app storage. They never leave your phone, except as part of your phone's own Android backup if you have turned that on, which Google handles under its own privacy policy.
  • The app has no ads, no analytics, no crash reporting, no accounts and no third-party code that collects data.
  • Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage deletes the count and label.

Children: the app is designed for a general audience and does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Changes: if this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this address with a new effective date.
