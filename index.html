Privacy Policy for Sri Nampally Baba Book Store

Last Updated: September 27, 2024

1. Introduction

Sri Nampally Baba Samsthan Trust ("we," "us," or "our") operates the Sri Nampally Baba Book Store mobile application (the "App"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our application.

Organization Details:

  • Owner: Sri Nampally Baba Samsthan Trust (SNB Trust)
  • Location: Dharmapuri Kshetram, Madeenaguda, Deepthi Sri Nagar, Miyapur, Hyderabad, Telangana, India

By using the App, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information You Provide

  • User Account Information: Username, password (encrypted), and role (administrator or seller)
  • Business Data: Book inventory details, customer information, sales transactions, event registrations
  • Customer Data: Names, contact information, purchase history (for business operations)

2.2 Automatically Collected Information

  • Device Information: Device model, operating system version
  • Usage Data: App features accessed, session duration
  • Log Data: Timestamps of transactions and activities within the app

2.3 Information We Do NOT Collect

  • We do not collect precise location data
  • We do not access your contacts, photos, or other personal files
  • We do not collect payment card information (all transactions are recorded manually)

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for:

  • Business Operations: Managing book inventory, tracking sales, generating reports
  • User Authentication: Securing access to the app with encrypted credentials
  • Customer Management: Maintaining customer records and purchase history
  • Event Management: Organizing and tracking book-related events and registrations
  • Analytics: Understanding app usage to improve functionality
  • Security: Detecting and preventing unauthorized access

4. Data Storage and Security

4.1 Data Storage

  • All data is stored in a secure cloud database (Turso/LibSQL)
  • Authentication tokens are encrypted using industry-standard encryption
  • User credentials are hashed and never stored in plain text

4.2 Security Measures

  • Encrypted communication between the app and server (HTTPS)
  • Secure storage using Android Keystore and EncryptedSharedPreferences
  • Access controls limiting data access to authorized users only
  • Regular security updates and patches

5. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do NOT sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties.

We may share information only in the following circumstances:

  • With Your Consent: When you explicitly authorize us to share information
  • Legal Requirements: When required by law, court order, or government regulation
  • Business Transfer: In the event of a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets

6. Data Retention

  • User Account Data: Retained while your account is active
  • Transaction Data: Retained for business records and accounting purposes (minimum 7 years as per business regulations)
  • Deleted Accounts: Data is permanently deleted within 30 days of account deletion request

7. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access: Request a copy of your personal data
  • Correction: Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Deletion: Request deletion of your account and associated data
  • Data Portability: Request your data in a machine-readable format
  • Opt-Out: Discontinue use of the app at any time

To exercise these rights, contact us at the details provided below.

8. Children's Privacy

This App is intended for business use and is not directed to children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

9. Third-Party Services

The App uses the following third-party services:

  • Cloud Database: Turso (LibSQL) for data storage
  • Image Loading: Glide library for displaying book covers

These services have their own privacy policies governing the use of your information.
